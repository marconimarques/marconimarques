### Hi there

The biggest winners of AI need not be the companies with the highest software budgets. They may be the companies with the lowest margins.

When margins are tight, everyday decisions carry extra weight. Better decisions can mean lower costs, less waste, and more profit.

Mathematical optimization can help businesses make those decisions by finding better ways to use their time and resources. But using solvers usually requires specialist knowledge, putting these tools beyond the reach of many professionals who could benefit from them.

### What if we could use these tools through a conversation?

We describe the scenario, work through the options, just as we would with a coworker.

That’s why I’m studying the intersection of LLMs and mathematical optimization. I’m exploring how natural language could make these tools accessible to more professionals, helping them turn everyday decisions into better business results. 

### What I Share
